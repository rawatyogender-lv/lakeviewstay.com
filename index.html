Stay where the mountains slow you down

Lake View Homestay is designed for families, couples and travellers looking for a calm base in the Garhwal hills. The property is conveniently located near the main highway while offering a quieter village setting away from the traffic.

Your stay

First bedroom with Kitchen

Triple-bed room with attached Kitchen and bathroom, geyser and air conditioning. A comfortable option for couples, families or small groups.

Second bedroom

A second triple-bed room with attached bathroom. For smaller bookings, room allocation may be based on the number of guests.

What you can enjoy

Lake & mountain views

Enjoy peaceful Himalayan scenery and lake views from the property.

🎲

Indoor games

Carrom, chess, ludo, snakes & ladders, UNO, cards and Jenga for relaxed evenings.

Simple kitchen access

Basic facilities are available for tea, coffee, Maggi, bread toast and simple roasted items.

🚗

Road access

The car can reach the property gate on the main road. Rooms are a short walk from the gate with stairs.

Wi‑Fi & essentials

Wi‑Fi and essential guest facilities are provided, subject to local network and power conditions.

Great travel base

Convenient for Srinagar, Dhari Devi Temple, Rudraprayag, Devprayag and onward Garhwal journeys.
